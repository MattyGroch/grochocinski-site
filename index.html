Matt Grochocinski

IT and security by day. Self-hosting and building things for the games I play by night.

Most of my free time goes into the server rack at home. It runs Mattyflix, a media server for my friends and family, plus game servers and a handful of apps I've built.

Most of those apps started as tools I wanted for myself: tracking story progress in Guild Wars 2, organizing a Star Wars: Unlimited collection, or a shared scoreboard for Marvel Champions game nights. When they turn out useful, I share them.

It goes back a long way. A few weeks after my dad brought home our family's first computer, an Acer with an Intel 486SX and 4 MB of RAM, I was already the family expert. My grandfather was a mentor from across the country, but mostly I taught myself, and I never stopped.

That turned into a career: ten years of running IT at tech companies, from IT support to Head of IT, and today IT & Security Manager. Knowing how systems are built and kept running, and how to keep them secure, is what makes the projects here work.

I like being the person with the answers, and I like sharing them even more. I'll never have all of them, and there will always be new problems to solve. I'm grateful for that.

Mattyflix

A private media server for friends and family

Mattyflix is the Plex server I run at home for friends and family: thousands of movies and shows, a site to request what's missing, a weekly newsletter of what's new, and a Discord community around it.

Behind it is a rack of NAS storage and a small Proxmox server running about 40 containers that find, download, organize and eventually clean up everything automatically.

  • Requests through Mattyflix Search, usually ready within a day
  • Personal watch stats, and a Rewind of your year every December
  • Invite only

Mattyflix Loot

Spare Steam keys, shared with the community

A vault for sharing spare Steam game keys with the Mattyflix community. Members sign in with Discord, link their Steam account, and spend credits to claim a game. I approve each request from a Discord DM.

It checks Steam so nobody gets a game they already own, keys are stored encrypted until they're delivered, and a broken key refunds its credit automatically.

  • Everyone starts with a credit and earns another every 30 days
  • Game details and art pulled straight from Steam
  • For Mattyflix members

GW2 Story Times

How long every Guild Wars 2 story mission takes

A community database of how long each Guild Wars 2 story mission takes, measured two ways: the full experience, with every cutscene and line of dialogue, and a speedrun.

It started with times gathered from YouTube walkthroughs and gets sharper with every run players submit. It has an open API, so anyone can build on it, and it powers the time estimates in GW2 Story Tracker and the BlishHUD module.

  • Full-experience and speedrun times for every mission
  • Open REST API at api.gw2storytimes.com

GW2 Story Tracker

Your story progress, and which character should finish what

Sign in with your Guild Wars 2 account and see your story journal progress across every character, from the Personal Story through the latest expansion.

It shows which character is closest to finishing each story and what to play next, with time estimates from GW2 Story Times. You can also share a link to your progress.

  • "Continue your journey" recommendations
  • Knows which expansions you own

Story Times for BlishHUD

Story mission times, right in the game

A Blish HUD module that brings GW2 Story Times into Guild Wars 2. A small overlay shows the current mission's time estimate, times your run with a built-in stopwatch, and submits your time to the community database in one click.

SWU Organizer

A Star Wars: Unlimited collection, binder by binder

Track a Star Wars: Unlimited collection the way it actually sits in your binders, slot by slot and printing by printing.

Scan cards in with your phone's camera, check decklists against what you own, and get walked through putting new cards away. It installs like an app, works offline, and syncs across devices.

  • Every printing tracked separately, from Normal to Prestige Serialized
  • Card scanner that recognizes the art, foils and Hyperspace included
  • Deck checking for Premier and Twin Suns

Marvel Champions LCG Companion

A shared scoreboard for game night

A digital scoreboard for Marvel Champions: The Card Game. The cards stay on the table, and the app tracks what's hard to read across it: villain HP, scheme threat, statuses and who's first player.

Friends join from their phones with a four-letter code, and every change shows up for everyone instantly. A TV mode, with Chromecast support, puts the whole board on the big screen.

  • No accounts needed for players
  • Any mis-tap can be undone
  • Hero, villain and scheme data synced from MarvelCDB
